Video doorbells that work without a subscription

14 doorbells sold in the UK, checked against manufacturer pages and independent reviews: what you get for free, what's behind a paywall, and what each one costs over three years.

Last checked 18 October 2026. No affiliate links, no tracking, no cookies. Prices change often, so each one links to its source.

The short answer

Best overall without fees: Reolink. It records to a card or NVR, nothing is paywalled, and it's the only one here with an official local Home Assistant integration.

Easiest battery pick: Tapo D230S1, which keeps footage on a hub inside the house.

Avoid if you won't pay monthly: Ring records nothing without a plan, and Blink and Arlo need extra hardware.

How we worked it out

a 3 years, no plan = price + anything you must add to record for free (Blink's Sync Module 2 at £39.99, Arlo's SmartHub at £99.74, and a microSD card or USB stick, assumed at £10.00; that last figure isn't from a cited source). Ring and Nest can't keep recordings this way, which is shown next to the figure.

b 3 years, with plan = price + 36 × the cheapest UK monthly plan that adds cloud recording for one device. Paying yearly is usually cheaper. The Reolink (£3.49) and EZVIZ (£4.99) plan prices aren't confirmed for the UK. Every model's sums are under Details.

Free-feature score (out of 8): records for free (2 = out of the box, 1 = with extra kit or only briefly), free live view (1), free motion alerts (1), free smart detection (1), storage included in the box (1), local Home Assistant support (2 = official and local, 1 = unofficial or generic).

Batteries, electricity, installation (except Hikvision, where the price includes fitting) and future price rises are left out. Anything we haven't fully confirmed is listed under Not yet verified in each model's details. Spotted something wrong, or a paywall change? Tell us and we'll fix it.

Product photos are the manufacturers' own, cropped and resized, from their product pages: Ring Battery Video Doorbell 2K, Google Nest Doorbell (battery), eufy Video Doorbell E340, Reolink Video Doorbell WiFi, Reolink Video Doorbell PoE, Reolink Video Doorbell (Battery), TP-Link Tapo D230S1, TP-Link Tapo TD21, Arlo Essential Video Doorbell 2K (2nd gen), Aqara Video Doorbell G4, Blink Battery Doorbell 2K+, EZVIZ EP3x Pro, EZVIZ DB2 Pro Kit.
