Short answer: which to buy

Already have a wired doorbell: Reolink WiFi, £93 (£119 over 3 yrs). Same wires, records to a card (or the PoE, £70, if you can run a cable).

Renting or no wires: Tapo D230S1, £85 (£110). Battery, footage kept indoors.

Cheapest that records free: Tapo TD21, £45 (£70). Basic, card sits in the doorbell.

Avoid if you won't pay monthly: Ring records nothing without a plan; Blink and Arlo need extra kit.

How we worked it out

a 3 years, no plan = price + anything you must add to record for free (Arlo's SmartHub at £105.07, Blink's Sync Module 2 at £39.99, and a 64 GB SanDisk microSD card at £25.37 or SanDisk USB stick at £11.99, each the cheapest UK price we found, on a page that publishes it). Ring and Nest can't keep recordings this way, which is shown next to the figure.

b 3 years, with plan = price + 36 × the cheapest UK monthly plan that adds cloud recording for one device. Paying yearly is usually cheaper. The EZVIZ plan figure is the EU price because EZVIZ shows UK pricing only inside its app. Every model's sums are under Details.

Free-feature score (out of 8): records for free (2 = out of the box, 1 = with extra kit or only briefly), free live view (1), free motion alerts (1), free smart detection (1), storage included in the box (1), local Home Assistant support (2 = official and local, 1 = unofficial or generic).

Plain terms. PoE (Power over Ethernet) sends power and video down one network cable. An NVR is a recorder box that stores footage from several cameras. A microSD card is the small memory card that sits inside many doorbells or their indoor chime. 2.4 GHz only means the doorbell cannot join a 5 GHz network, so it will not show up on a phone that is on 5 GHz. Home Assistant is free software that keeps your smart home on your own network instead of a manufacturer's cloud.

Batteries, electricity, installation (except Hikvision, where the price includes fitting) and future price rises are left out. Anything not yet confirmed is listed under Not yet verified in each model's details.
