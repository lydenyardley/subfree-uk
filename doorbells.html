Video doorbells that work without a subscription

14 doorbells sold in the UK, checked against manufacturer pages and independent reviews: what you get for free, what's behind a paywall, and what each one costs over three years.

Last checked 19 October 2026. No affiliate links, no tracking, no cookies. Prices change often, so each one links to its source. Spotted a mistake? Open an issue.

Short answer: Reolink PoE or Tapo D230S1. Ring needs a plan to record.

The short answer

Best overall without fees: Reolink Video Doorbell PoE. It records to a card or NVR, nothing is paywalled, and Reolink is the only brand here with an official local Home Assistant integration. The WiFi and battery versions follow the same rules if you can't run an Ethernet cable.

Easiest battery pick: Tapo D230S1, which keeps footage on a hub inside the house.

Avoid if you won't pay monthly: Ring records nothing without a plan, and Blink and Arlo need extra hardware.

How we worked it out

a 3 years, no plan = price + anything you must add to record for free (Blink's Sync Module 2 at £39.99, Arlo's SmartHub at £105.07, and a microSD card or USB stick, assumed at £10.00; that last figure isn't from a cited source). Ring and Nest can't keep recordings this way, which is shown next to the figure.

b 3 years, with plan = price + 36 × the cheapest UK monthly plan that adds cloud recording for one device. Paying yearly is usually cheaper. The EZVIZ plan figure is the EU price because EZVIZ shows UK pricing only inside its app. Every model's sums are under Details.

Free-feature score (out of 8): records for free (2 = out of the box, 1 = with extra kit or only briefly), free live view (1), free motion alerts (1), free smart detection (1), storage included in the box (1), local Home Assistant support (2 = official and local, 1 = unofficial or generic).

Plain terms. PoE (Power over Ethernet) sends power and video down one network cable. An NVR is a recorder box that stores footage from several cameras. A microSD card is the small memory card that sits inside many doorbells or their indoor chime. Home Assistant is free software that keeps your smart home on your own network instead of a manufacturer's cloud.

Batteries, electricity, installation (except Hikvision, where the price includes fitting) and future price rises are left out. Anything we haven't fully confirmed is listed under Not yet verified in each model's details. Spotted something wrong, or a paywall change? Open an issue and we'll fix it.
