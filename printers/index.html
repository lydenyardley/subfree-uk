How we worked it out

a 3 years, retail ink = the printer's price + 36 months of original cartridges at 100 pages a month (50 mono, 50 colour), priced from the maker's own UK cartridge pages and ISO yields. A tank printer is priced at its price alone, because the bottles in the box cover the whole period. Starter cartridges are left out of both routes, so a cartridge figure is an upper bound.

b 3 years, with an ink plan = the printer's price + 36 × the cheapest UK monthly plan that covers 100 pages. A printer with no plan to price says No plan needed, because its bottles are the ink route. Every printer's sums are under Details.

No-plan score (out of 8): prints on retail ink without an account (2 = standard cartridges or bottles, 1 = original ink only, 0 = a plan or account is required), print speed (1), automatic double-sided printing (1), scan and copy (1), ink included in the box (1), prints without an account (2 = no account at all, 1 = an app is optional, 0 = an account is required).

Plain terms. An ink plan is a monthly payment that posts cartridges or tops up a tank before you run out. A tank printer has refillable reservoirs and comes with bottles instead of cartridges. Yield is the number of pages a cartridge or bottle prints to the ISO/IEC standard. Duplex means printing on both sides of the sheet.

Paper, electricity, delivery and future price rises are left out. Anything not yet confirmed is listed under Not yet verified in each printer's details.
