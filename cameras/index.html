The short answer

Best overall without fees: Reolink RLC-820A. It records 4K to a card or NVR, detection is free and Reolink's Home Assistant integration is fully local. It needs a PoE switch or injector and an Ethernet run to the camera.

Best wire-free pick: Reolink Argus 4 Pro, which covers 180° from one battery camera and still records to a card without a plan.

Avoid if you won't pay monthly: Ring records nothing without a plan, and Blink needs a Sync Module 2 and a USB stick before it saves a clip.

How we worked it out

a 3 years, no plan = price + anything you must add to record for free (Blink's Sync Module 2 at £39.99, Blink's Sync Module 2 at £39.99, and a 64 GB SanDisk microSD card at £26.99 or SanDisk USB stick at £15.99, each the cheapest UK price we found, on a page that publishes it). Cameras with no free recording are shown next to the figure.

b 3 years, with plan = price + 36 × the cheapest UK monthly plan that adds cloud recording for one camera. Paying yearly is usually cheaper. A camera that records free without a plan is still priced against its optional one, so the two columns compare like with like. Every model's sums are under Details.

Free-feature score (out of 8): records for free (2 = out of the box, 1 = with extra kit or only briefly), free live view (1), free motion alerts (1), free smart detection (1), storage included in the box (1), local Home Assistant support (2 = official and local, 1 = unofficial or generic).

Plain terms. PoE (Power over Ethernet) sends power and video down one network cable. An NVR is a recorder box that stores footage from several cameras. A microSD card is the small memory card that sits inside many cameras or their hub. 2.4 GHz only means the camera cannot join a 5 GHz network, so it will not show up on a phone that is on 5 GHz. Home Assistant is free software that keeps your smart home on your own network instead of a manufacturer's cloud.

Batteries, electricity, installation and future price rises are left out. Anything not yet confirmed is listed under Not yet verified in each model's details.
