## How the 3-year costs are worked out
- Without a plan: the tracker's price, plus any collar or holder it needs. A tracker whose core function needs a plan is shown at its price alone, and the table says it does not track without one.
- With a plan: price + 36 × the cheapest UK monthly plan that keeps it working. Paying yearly is cheaper: Tractive's Basic year is £84, Pawfit's £71.88 and Weenect's yearly rate is £6.67 a month.
- A collar, roaming data, batteries, charging and price rises are left out.
