- Without a plan: the monitor's price, because the free tier covers live viewing and alerts on every row. A local monitor sends nothing to the maker; a smart monitor needs its app and servers for live view.
- With the plan: price + 36 × the cheapest UK monthly plan that adds the paywalled extras (Nanit Memories at £10). A monitor with no plan to price says so; Owlet360 is priced only in its app.
- Electricity, a phone or tablet, microSD cards and price rises are left out.
