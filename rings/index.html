The short answer

Best overall without a subscription: RingConn Gen 3. It tracks sleep, HRV, SpO2, temperature and overnight vascular trends, lasts up to 14 days and adds nothing to the price. It has no Home Assistant integration.

Best value: RingConn Gen 2, which keeps its data free for £159, adds the sleep-apnea insights the Air leaves out, and lasts up to 12 days on a charge.

Avoid if you won't pay monthly: Oura Ring 5 shows only three daily scores without its membership; the sleep stages, HRV, SpO2 and trends need £5.99 a month. The Ring 4 is the same ring a generation back, for £244.

How we worked it out

a 3 years, no subscription = the ring's price, because no maker charges for the app itself. What the free tier keeps differs: Oura keeps three daily scores, and the other rings keep their full data. Each row says which.

b 3 years, with membership = the price + 36 × the cheapest monthly membership. Only Oura charges one: £5.99 a month, or £69.99 a year, which works out cheaper. A ring with no membership says so in that column, so the two totals compare like with like. Every ring's sums are under Details.

Where to buy. Each ring is sold by more than one UK shop, and its price moves with size and finish, so every offer names the one it is for. The price shown is the cheapest we found, unless a second shop is within £5 of it and pays us more — then that shop is shown, and the link on the price goes to it. A shop paying us never adds more than £5 to the price of a ring.

Free-data score (out of 8): works without a subscription (2 = every core metric free, 1 = scores only or a free trial, 0 = a membership is required), sleep detail free (1), heart rate free (1), SpO2 and temperature free (1), advanced insights free (1), local Home Assistant support (2 = official and local, 1 = unofficial or cloud).

Plain terms. A smart ring is a finger-worn tracker with no screen: it sends what it measures to a phone app. HRV (heart-rate variability) is the variation between heartbeats, used as a recovery signal. SpO2 is blood-oxygen saturation. A membership is the subscription some brands charge before the app shows its detailed data. Home Assistant is free software that keeps your smart home on your own network instead of a manufacturer's cloud.

A phone, electricity, sizing kits and future price rises are left out. Anything not yet confirmed is listed under Not yet verified in each ring's details.
