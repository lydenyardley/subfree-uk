Arrange the comparison

Each column carries a handle on its rule. Drag the handle and the column takes its neighbour's place, or send it to either end. The whole column moves with it, the cost row included, and the figures stay put: the cheapest model is the one the other two are measured against.

Arrow keys move the column one place. Home and End send it to either end. Escape puts the columns back in the order the page opened with.

What to judge
  • The handle: does the clip on the rule read as something you can drag, without a caption?
  • The motion: does the column land where you expect, and does its cost cell carry the delta with it?
  • The arithmetic: the cheapest of the three is the reference, so moving a column reorders the reading and leaves every figure where it was.
  • The share line: it now counts rows where all three models agree, so no difference is hidden.
  • The keyboard: each handle is a button and a tab stop, with Left, Right, Home, End and Escape.
  • Reduced motion: the columns land without sliding, and the deltas still recompute.
  • Scope: this is the desktop picture. The phone keeps its cards.
