The comparison instrument

Pull the divider and the next model comes into the comparison. The deltas and the rows the two models share follow it. A still table can only show one pair; this shows which pair you are on.

Side-by-side comparison of 3 doorbells, with a divider that chooses which model is compared against the first

Arrow keys swap the compared model. Home and End choose one. Escape returns to the first pair.

What to judge
  • The gesture: does the divider read as something you can pull, and does the model land where you expect?
  • The arithmetic: the delta chips name the baseline, and the collapsed line counts the rows the pair shares.
  • The keyboard: the divider is one tab stop, 44px wide, with Left, Right, Home, End and Escape.
  • Reduced motion: the columns land without sliding, and the deltas still recompute.
