# Impeccable delight: SubFree UK

- Target: `index.html`, the 14-row UK video doorbell comparison
- Run: 19 October 2026, read-only
- Mode: Read plus Operate. One or two visits, often on a phone, ending in a
  purchase decision.
- Persona: Casey, the distracted phone reader, plus Jordan on first use.

## Why this surface earns delight

The reader is about to spend £100 to £300 and is surrounded by review sites
that repeat a spec sheet. The page's advantage is not cheer. It is the feeling
of being accompanied by someone who did the arithmetic and will say when a
number is uncertain. The emotional job is confidence under ambiguity.

Delight here must live in evidence, not confetti. A celebration on a filter
click would undercut the one thing the page is selling.

## Delight thesis

The reader should feel that the page did the homework with them, not for them:
every number is traceable, every gap is named out loud, and the verdict arrives
like a note from a careful friend who showed their working.

That thesis is already half built. The cost row computes the three-year total
and the difference. The paragraph on the Ring price says the £10 figure is not
from a cited source. Those are the memorable moves, and neither is decoration.

This run inherits the accepted guardrails in
`docs/decisions/0001-delight-guardrails.md`: the four personality moments, the
quiet zones, the verification contract and the byte budget. Its thesis narrows
the same idea toward evidence rather than replacing it.

## Opportunity map

| Moment | Current treatment | Delight potential |
|---|---|---|
| First viewport | Eyebrow, title, last-checked date, verdict, six key numbers | Strong. The verdict reads as a person's conclusion, not a label. |
| Filtering and sorting | Instant table change, count line, jump announcement | Low. Correctly quiet. |
| Reading a row | Sources, notes, cost breakdown in Details | Strong. The receipts are the character. |
| Comparing | Pinned tray with three-year cost and difference | Strongest moment on the page, and it already lands. |
| Empty result | Names the nearest real model and offers Reset | Strong. Empathy plus a way out. |
| Copying a shortlist | Copies Markdown, announces success | Underplayed. This is the one shareable artifact. |
| Leaving | 52 sources, method, glossary | Weak as an ending. It reads as an archive, not a handoff. |

## What is genuinely delightful now

**The confession.** "That £10 is an estimate, not from a cited source." Most
commercial pages would drop the number or bury the caveat. Saying it in the
sentence that uses it makes the rest of the page more believable. A
neighbouring product could not reuse this unchanged.

**The arithmetic in the compare tray.** The page does not just list prices; it
multiplies by three years and subtracts. The reader's job shifts from
calculator to judge. That is a small act of care with a large effect on
confidence.

**The recovery state.** "Nothing matches those filters" plus a named model at
£295 is warmer and more useful than an apology. It treats the dead end as a
chance to keep helping.

**The record marker.** A half-filled square for "needs kit" is a better
metaphor than a tick or a cross, and it survives without a legend.

## Where delight is left on the table

The page builds confidence and then ends in a bibliography. The final third is
proof, and proof is good, but it is not a sendoff. The reader who has just
decided between two models gets a source list and a footer.

The copy action is the natural sendoff and is currently silent about its
value. It copies Markdown and says "Copied". It does not say what was copied
or why it is useful.

The score bars are the page's only motion tied to sorting, and they animate on
every sort, which is the least emotional moment. The bar draw would mean more
when the table first comes into view, where it would read as the page measuring
the options, and less on every re-sort.

## Numbered suggestions

Two of the six shipped on 20 October 2026 and are pruned from this list: the
copy action now names what it copied and what for, in a status line that
fades, and the source list sits in a host-grouped disclosure on narrow
screens. Both are recorded in
`docs/decisions/0002-labels-sources-and-compare.md`.

1. Add a closing line after the verdict that names the trade the reader is
   making, for example the cheapest three-year cost versus the model tagged
   "Easiest battery setup". One sentence, no new data, no claim that is not
   already on the page.
2. Draw the score bars once, when the table first comes into view, and make
   later sorts instant. One authored moment tied to reading the rows, not 14
   on every interaction.
3. Make the nearest-model button state its action. It currently says "Show X
   at £Y" and works by widening the price sliders. Say "Widen the budget to
   £Y to include X", so the reader knows the power and record filters stay put.
4. Add a compact "Back to the short answer" link after the last row, before
   the method. The page was 8,720px on a phone at the time of this audit and
   the verdict is at the top; one line is enough, not a floating button.

## Built for the emotional moments

- **Success.** The compare cost row is the success moment and it is already
  proportional: no animation, just the number and the difference. Keep it
  that way.
- **Waiting.** Nothing on the page fakes work. The only wait is the page
  loading, which is a single inline document. No skeleton screens needed.
- **Empty and first use.** The reader sees the verdict and the six key numbers
  before any controls. That is the right order. The empty filter state does
  not dead-end.
- **Error and recovery.** The price-assumption note is the model for every
  future caveat: lead with the fact, name the limit, keep going.
- **Repeat use.** Filters and sort are instant and do not celebrate. That is
  correct for a page used twice.
- **Discovery.** The Details disclosures and the cost breakdown reward a
  curious reader without hiding required information.

## Protect the experience

- Do not add sound, confetti or a mascot. The page's voice is dry and precise;
  whimsy would read as a different product.
- Do not delay the table to stage the bar draw.
- Do not add a claim that is not already sourced. Delight copy must not
  introduce new facts.
- Keep the reduced-motion path instant. The bar draw is decorative.
- Keep the copy confirmation in a live region, and do not steal focus.

## Verify

- The thesis is specific to this page: a neighbouring shopping comparison
  could not use "we showed our working" unchanged without the confession and
  the cost row.
- Every suggestion improves confidence, orientation or recovery. None adds a
  task.
- The page is fast and obvious with the flourishes removed.
- Repetition does not turn charm into friction: the copy line fades, the bars
  draw once, the closing line is static.
- Keyboard, touch, reduced motion and screen-reader paths all remain intact.
