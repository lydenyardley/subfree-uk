# The 23 October 2026 design critique

- Status: Closed. All fifteen settled on 24 October (ADR 22): eleven as
  found, R5's alignment without its verdicts, R13 as drawn, R6 rejected, R1
  not taken.
- Date: 23 October 2026
- Target: `index.html`, `build/page.css`, `build/page.js`
- Method: two isolated assessments (design review from source and pixels; the
  bundled detector, axe-core and DOM measurement), then a third pass that
  re-measured every contested claim against the served working tree. The
  numbers below are from that third pass.

## Context

The page is a single static comparison of 14 UK video doorbells. It answers one
question: what records without a subscription, and what does three years cost
with and without a plan. The findings cluster where the reader spends money or
checks a claim.

The audit covered 1280px light and dark, 390px light, the compare panel with
three models, an open details panel, the empty filter state, no JavaScript,
print, and a cold load at 4x CPU throttle.

## What works

1. **One colour language across three surfaces.** The short-answer bars, the
   3px row edge and the compare column rule share `--v-best`, `--v-batt` and
   `--v-avoid`.
2. **The comparison leads with the buyer's question.** "Cheapest way to record
   for 3 years" puts the total first, the delta second, the basis third.
3. **The empty state names the cause.** "No doorbell costs between £60 and £60.
   The closest is Reolink Video Doorbell PoE at £69.99", with a one-tap fix.

## What was rejected

These came from one assessment or the other and did not survive re-measurement.
They are recorded so a later pass does not reopen them.

- **The detector's 13 warnings are not defects.** The 10 `side-tab` findings
  are the 3px verdict rule. `all-caps-body` is the 40-character masthead
  tagline. `flat-type-hierarchy` compared 12px and 13px labels and ignored the
  44.8px h1. `aphoristic-cadence` names one factual disclosure.
- **The full-page screenshot's blank table is a capture artifact.**
  `content-visibility: auto` leaves off-screen cards out of the capture.
- **The first product is not below the fold.** The first card starts at 580px
  in an 844px viewport.
- **The Reolink price links are not false.** Both rows link to the listing that
  `rows.py` records as `price_src`.
- **The shortlist is not missing provenance.** It carries `shareUrl()`, and the
  page it links to carries the sources.
- **Dark mode's photo dimming is deliberate.** ADR 16.
- **The 24px chevron was not a 24px target.** Its `::after` gave it 44x44; the
  affordance was small, not the target.
- **Footnote links are inline.** WCAG 2.2's inline exception covers them; the
  desktop compare box was a different case because it sat alone in a rail.
- **Side-by-side on a phone stays.** ADR 7 and ADR 8 declined stacking; R2 asks
  for a truthful cue and a snap, not a different structure.

## Open questions, answered

1. TD21 carries no verdict: the owner kept the alignment and dropped the
   words, so the sort order and the copy are unchanged.
2. "Side by side" on a phone does show one model at a time, so the cue and the
   snap now say so; the copy is unchanged.
3. The no-JS reader is in scope: the panels are open in the HTML.
